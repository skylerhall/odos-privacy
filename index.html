ODOS Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · Android app com.skylerhall.odos
Developer: Egghead Development · Contact: skylerchall@gmail.com

The short version: ODOS has no accounts, no ads, no analytics of its own, and no server of its own. The developer does not receive, store, or sell your location or any other personal information. To do its job the app sends only what each request needs to the map, weather and places services listed below, directly from your phone.

Information the app uses

Your location

With your permission, ODOS uses your phone's precise location (GPS) while the app is open to show you on the map, give turn-by-turn directions, track fuel use, and find gas, rest stops and weather near you or along your route. ODOS does not use your location in the background when the app is closed.

You can say no, or turn location off later in your phone's settings. ODOS still opens, and you can still search places and drop pins on the map, but navigation from where you are won't work.

To answer a request, ODOS sends coordinates (your position, your destination, a dropped pin, or points along your route) to these services:

What you type

  • Place searches (for example an address or "coffee") go to Mapbox Search to find matching places.
  • Vehicle details (year, make, model, engine) go to the U.S. Department of Energy's fueleconomy.gov to look up fuel economy. If you choose to enter a VIN, it goes to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's vPIC service to decode it. ODOS does not keep the VIN after the lookup.

Mapbox map data (telemetry)

The Mapbox map built into ODOS may send Mapbox anonymous location and usage data, which Mapbox uses to improve its maps and traffic. Mapbox says this data is de-identified, and that it doesn't collect advertising identifiers. You can turn this off at any time: tap the (i) button next to the Mapbox logo on the map, then choose the telemetry option. See mapbox.com/telemetry.

What stays on your phone

ODOS saves these only on your device, so they're still there next time you open the app: your vehicle and fuel level, your shortcuts (such as Home and Work) and recent places, your settings (such as night mode, voice, weather effects and avoiding tolls), and offline maps and nearby places saved for your current trip. Uninstalling ODOS, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes all of it. Spoken directions use your phone's built-in text-to-speech, and the automatic night map works out sunset and sunrise on your phone from your location, without sending it anywhere.

Sharing your location with others

When you tap Share location or Copy coordinates, ODOS creates a message with your coordinates and a map link, and hands it to the app you choose (or your clipboard). It only happens when you tap, and it goes only where you send it.

What ODOS does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers, or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.
  • No location use while the app is closed.

Security

Every request ODOS makes uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

The services ODOS uses

Each service handles the data it receives under its own privacy policy: Mapbox (mapbox.com/legal/privacy), Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com/en/terms), the U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov), fueleconomy.gov (U.S. Department of Energy), NHTSA vPIC (nhtsa.gov), the OpenStreetMap Overpass API (overpass-api.de) and Recreation.gov.

Children

ODOS is a driving app for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
